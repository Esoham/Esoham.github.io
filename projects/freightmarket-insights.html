Suggested account-management workflow

  1. Validate the customer's reported issue against available shipment and billing records.
  2. Identify owner and response deadline; communicate next update to customer.
  3. Resolve the immediate issue, document root cause, and review repeated patterns.
  4. Escalate recurring issues into a process-improvement proposal with measurable follow-up.

Methods: descriptive segmentation, aging analysis, recurring-issue classification. This is a learning prototype, not a predictive model or production CRM.
