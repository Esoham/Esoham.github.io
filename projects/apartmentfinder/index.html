Authorized rental-data feed RentCast

Checking whether a rental feed is connected…

Saved rentals remain on this device only, without an account. Provider records may disappear or change after refresh. Neighborhood links are map searches, not verified listing advertisements.

A provider's “Active” status is not proof an individual apartment is still available. Low listed prices may apply to a single room rather than an entire home; confirm what is rented. The source may omit application fees, deposits, utilities and pet rules, so ApartmentFinder never guesses those costs for provider listings. Refreshes are periodic, not real-time.

Renter budget planner

What will life actually cost here?

Choose a real rental above or enter a rent manually. No account needed.

Choose “Plan costs” on a rental listing above, or enter a monthly rent below.

All expenses and fees are user-entered, including transportation, groceries, and debt. A $0 field means none entered, not confirmed free. RentCast does not supply complete fees. This planner calculates cash flow using your monthly take-home income, not a lender's or landlord's income qualification formula. It does not save or transmit your financial entries. Leave room for savings and emergencies, and verify rent, lease terms and all fees before paying anyone.

What do the estimates include?

      

        
01
Monthly rent
The sample advertised rent for the home. Actual properties may charge additional recurring fees.

        
02
Estimated monthly total
Rent + sample utilities estimate + optional parking. Pet rent is added when you choose a pet-friendly unit with a pet-rent amount.

        
03
Estimated move-in cost
First month's rent + sample security deposit + application fee + administrative fee. It excludes taxes, pet deposits, insurance, prorating, and other possible charges.
